## Art direction

Realistic miniature objects in ivory ceramic/paper, matte graphite, brushed aluminum, and restrained copper. The community asset is the shared lighting/material reference. No purple, lavender, neon, or sparkles. Utilities such as arrows and checkmarks remain text for small-size legibility.
